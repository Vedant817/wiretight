01 / About

Software agents are learning to track balances, initiate payments, and manage risk without a human in the loop.

The rails underneath those agents are yours to design. Over 48 hours, teams build on Airwallex APIs — global accounts, FX, payouts, cards, and transactional data — to ship agents that run real financial workflows.

You'll get sandbox access, mentors from the Airwallex engineering team, and a demo day in front of judges from fintech and venture. Regardless of your experience, if you're excited about agents and money movement, you're in the right place.

06 / FAQ

Questions

Who can take part?

Developers, designers, data scientists, and founders aged 18 and over, anywhere in the world. If you’re excited about agents and money movement, we encourage you to apply.

Does it cost anything?

No. Entry is free, and every registered participant gets access to the Airwallex sandbox and workshops.

Can I enter solo or do I need a team?

Both work. Enter on your own or form a team of up to four. Find teammates in the community Discord before kickoff.

Do I keep the IP for what I build?

Yes. You retain full ownership of your project. Submitting only grants us permission to showcase your work as part of the hackathon.

What do I need to submit?

A working demo, a short video walkthrough under five minutes, and a link to your repository with setup instructions.

Do I have to attend in person?

No. The hackathon runs online with an optional in-person demo day in San Francisco. Mentors and judging are available to remote teams too.
